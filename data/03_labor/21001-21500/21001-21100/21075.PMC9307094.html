Correction to: Temporal Trends of Infective Endocarditis in North America From 2000 to 2017—A Systematic Review

In the original publication of this article, [Talha KM, Dayer JM, Thornhill MH et al. Temporal Trends of Infective Endocarditis in North America From 2000 to 2017—A Systematic Review. Open Forum Infectious Diseases, Volume 8, Issue 11, November 2021. https://doi.org/10.1093/ofid/ofab479] the authors erroneously stated that the Toyoda 2017 and Mori 2020 studies in Table 4 used ICD-10 Code Validation. This has been removed in the version currently available online.
