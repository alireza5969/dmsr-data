Autologous Breast Reconstruction after Mastectomy for Breast Cancer: A Systematic Review—Erratum

The article by Saldanha et al, published as e4181 in Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery Global Open on March 14, 2022, was originally published with the wrong title, “Autologous Reconstruction after Mastectomy for Breast Cancer.” The correct title is “Autologous Breast Reconstruction after Mastectomy for Breast Cancer: A Systematic Review.”

This how now been corrected on the website.
