Author notes:

Correspondence to: [⁎] Corresponding author.

We wish to provide a clarification of the labels of the forest plot, Fig. 3, in the published study. The SD column should be labelled as the “standard error of the mean difference”. We provide a detailed explanation of what Fig. 3 columns refer to:

  • (1) Mean: This refers to the mean difference of the group scores which was calculated as the pre-treatment score minus post treatment score.

  • (2) SD: This refers to the standard error of the mean (SEM) difference which was calculated as SQRT (SEM of the pre-treatment score^2+SEM of the post-treatment score ^2). The SEM for pre and post treatment scores was calculated as SD/SQRT of sample size.

  • (3) Total: This is the sample size in each study arm

  • (4) The final weight and the SMD IV, Random, 95%CI were generated by RevMan.

Forest Plot for symptoms severity. Corrected Choi and Wright studies. Mean refers to the difference between pre and post treatment mean. SD refers to SEM. eCBT electronic CBT, fCBT face to face CBT. Experimental=eCBT, Control=face to face CBT.

We acknowledge an error in numbers extracted from Choi et al. 2014 which provided standard error and not SD (now correctly calculated from the SE using this formula: SD= SE x SQRT of sample size) and Wright et al. 2005 post treatment numbers were incorrectly extracted. We provide the data file with corrected numbers. We re-did the meta-analysis with the corrected numbers, the results continued to be consistent and in the same direction as in the review (original overall estimate in the meta-analysis of the depression severity score from Fig. 3 was (−1.73, 95% CI −2.72, −0.74, I2 98%), revised analysis (−0.92, 95% CI −1.76, −0.09, I2 97%)). This error did not change the results, direction, or the conclusions.
